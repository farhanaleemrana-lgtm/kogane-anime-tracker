About Kōgane

Kōgane is an independent, fan-built anime catalog and tracker. The name comes from the Japanese word for "gold" — a nod to the site's visual theme and to the idea of a well-curated collection worth returning to.

What This Site Is

Kōgane brings together anime titles across genres — action, fantasy, romance, slice of life, and more — with cover art, ratings, studio and production details, and genre tags, all in one searchable, filterable place.

Where the Data Comes From

Catalog information is drawn from the community-maintained Anime Offline Database, which itself aggregates data from sources including MyAnimeList, AniList, Kitsu, and AniDB. Cover art is displayed from these same sources or hosted locally where available.

What This Site Isn't

Kōgane does not host, stream, or distribute anime episodes or manga chapters. It is a catalog and discovery tool, not a viewing platform. Links and information are provided for reference; official viewing and reading should go through licensed platforms.
